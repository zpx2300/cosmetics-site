The line

This is where the cartons come from

Produced by Guangzhou Run-the source Cosmetics Co., Ltd (广州药妆日用化工有限公司) — the plant we order from, in Guangzhou. Not stock photography: this is the filling line, the clean room and the showroom. Your cartons leave this plant and clear into Ghana.

Your order is produced in Guangzhou, cleared and delivered into Accra. One contact at this end handles the paperwork, the clearing and the handover — you are not chasing a factory at 3 a.m.

Own label

Your name on the pack — when the volume is there

We are not going to pretend otherwise: at fifty or a hundred pieces the factory will not stop the line to print your artwork. So we start the honest way — you sell their packs, you learn what moves, and when the numbers justify it we open up your own label.

  • 1Your logo and design — your artwork, or we build the label with you
  • 2Your pack — bottle, jar, tube; sizes chosen for your price point
  • 3Your formula — texture, scent and actives adjusted at volume
  • 4What it takes — a volume commitment and a deposit. That is what makes the run worth setting up

If you are placing a large order and can commit with a deposit, tell us early — the artwork and the proof take longer than the production.

Who we are

A Ghana-side team, working directly with the line

We are building this business in Ghana one customer at a time. We ask before we buy: which categories move, at which price points, in which pack sizes. That conversation decides what we carry — and it is why the range on this page is short and specific rather than a thousand items nobody asked for.

Because the order goes to the factory rather than through a chain of importers, you get the product you actually specified, at a price that leaves you room — and a direct line to the people who print the packs when you are ready for your own.
